Guide

Color Basics

HEX: A compact online color code, like #7C3AED.
RGB: Red, green, and blue values that make a color.
HSL: Hue, saturation, and lightness. Useful for adjusting color by feel.

Image Types

Single: One solid color.
Gradient: A smooth blend between two colors.
Palette: A set of separate colors with no blending.
Gradient Palette: A stepped palette between two colors.
Batch: Ten generated options shown at once.

Styles

Any: Fully random colors.
Bright: Vivid, lively colors.
Dark: Low-light colors.
Pastel: Light, gentle colors.
Muted: Toned-down, low-intensity colors.
Neon: Electric high-saturation colors.
Warm: Reds, oranges, and yellows.
Cool: Blues, greens, and purples.
Earth: Clay, sand, brown, olive, and natural tones.
Ocean: Aqua, teal, blue, and navy.
Sunset: Orange, pink, purple, and gold.

Vintage: Faded, dusty, retro colors.
Contrast: Colors with strong separation.
Monochrome: One hue family with light/dark variation.
