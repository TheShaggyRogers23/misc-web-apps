Basics

Single: One solid color.
Gradient: A smooth blend between two colors.
Palette: Separate color bands.
Gradient Palette: Stepped bands between two colors.
Batch: Generates 9 random colors, gradients, palettes, or gradient palettes at a time.
Favorites: Saves chosen colors, gradients, palettes, or gradient palettes for later reuse.
History: Stores recent Random results automatically so they can be recovered without saving them as Favorites.
